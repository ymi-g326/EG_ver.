## Processing in the application

The recordings are used as timbral material rather than represented as live audio from the ISS subpoint. The active recording is split into four filtered bands for MOTIF, PAD, BASS, and PULSE. Web Audio gain envelopes expose short or sustained fragments on the generative rhythm grid. Surface changes use a gradual crossfade. Deterministic synth voices remain underneath to preserve pitch, scale, and harmonic structure.

The checked-in MP3 files are Freesound's public preview encodes of the CC0 recordings. They must not be described as recordings captured at the current ISS location.
